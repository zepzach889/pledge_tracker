Tick "No pledge" for breaks and no-school weeks ahead of time; the rotation picks up the next week where it left off. If a leader is absent, the backup reads the Pledge and the original leader moves into the next week's slot, so nobody loses their turn.

Each row is one turn: a solo student, a friend pair, or a random pair from the any-partner group (new partners each time through). The rotation runs top to bottom, then starts over. Pause a student who is out for a while; their partner reads alone. Remove only students who have withdrawn.
